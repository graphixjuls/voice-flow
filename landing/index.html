How it works

Three steps, no
setup ritual.

01

Hold Ctrl+Alt

Anywhere in Windows — a text field, a chat box, a code comment. No app to switch to first.

02

Say what you mean

A small floating widget turns red while it's listening, so you always know it's live.

03

Let go

Whisper transcribes it in under a second and the text is pasted right where your cursor was.

Why it's different

Built to disappear
into your workflow

Private by default

Local mode runs OpenAI's Whisper model entirely on your CPU. Audio never touches the internet.

Cloud mode when you need it

Flip one toggle to route through OpenAI's transcription API for noisy rooms or tricky accents.

Works in any app

No plugins or integrations to install per-app — it pastes into whatever currently has focus.

Free & open source

The full source is on GitHub. Read it, change it, or build it yourself from a clone.
